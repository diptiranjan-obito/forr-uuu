THERE AREN'T ENOUGH WORDS FOR YOU.

I tried to find the right words for you.
I couldn't.
So I made this instead.

She is not a description. She is a feeling.

I may never know every sacrifice you've made,
every quiet thing you've done,
or every moment you've chosen to care without being noticed.

But I know this:

The goodness you carry doesn't suddenly appear when someone is watching.

Some things aren't decided in front of people. They are built in the unseen hours, in small choices, over and over, until they become who someone is.

some light is made far from the spotlight

Some people become friends.
Some people slowly become a place that feels like home.

Someone who is there. Someone who listens. Someone who remembers the small things.

Someone who makes you feel a little less alone just by being in the conversation. Someone you trust without having to decide to.

And the small talks, the ones you'd never think to call important, turn out to be the ones you keep.

Somewhere along the way, your presence changed my days. I don't know exactly when. I only know they're different now.

Someone worth listening to.

Someone far more precious than she sometimes believes.

Things that were too big for a normal conversation.

Some feelings refuse to fit inside ordinary sentences.

So I gave them a little poetry.

for the girl who somehow makes words feel insufficient ✦

I started making this because I wanted to explain why you're precious to me.

I tried describing you as a girl.
Then as a daughter.
Then as a friend.
Then simply as a person.

And somehow…

I still don't think I've managed to explain it.

Maybe some people are simply too good
to fit into a paragraph.

You're one of them.
